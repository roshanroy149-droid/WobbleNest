Why Wobblenest

Built to slow swiping down, on purpose.

Every feature exists to get you to a real conversation faster — not to keep you swiping longer.

Intention-first matching

Tell us what you're actually looking for — casual, serious, something in between — and we match you against people who want the same thing.

Your photos, your rules

Keep certain photos private by default. Someone has to ask, and you decide who gets to see them — not the algorithm.

Shared interests, surfaced

We highlight exactly what you have in common with someone before you match — so the spark has something real to start from.

Conversations that start right

No more staring at a blank message box. Get icebreakers built from what you actually share with the person you just matched with.

How it works

Three steps to something real.

1

Set your intention

Tell us what kind of connection you're looking for, and what matters to you — before you see a single profile.

2

Discover with purpose

Browse profiles matched on intention and shared interests, not just proximity and a good photo.

3

Connect for real

Match, break the ice with a suggestion built for you two, and take the conversation from there.

Why "Wobblenest"?

A nest doesn't get built in one swipe. It takes a little wobble, some trial and error, and two people actually choosing to build something together. That's the kind of dating app we set out to make — one that's honest about the process, not just the outcome.
