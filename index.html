What's inside

Eighteen things that make it different.

Every one of them exists to get you to a real conversation faster — not to keep you swiping longer.

Dating, done differently

Matching that starts from what you want

Intention-first matching

Say what you're looking for — casual, serious, somewhere in between — before you see a single profile. You're matched against people who want the same.

Tap, don't swipe

You decide with a tap, on a card that shows a whole person rather than one cropped photo. No streaks, no timers, no pressure to keep going.

Shared interests, surfaced

We show you exactly what you have in common with someone before you match — so the spark has something real to start from.

Icebreakers that fit

No more staring at a blank message box. Openers are built from what you and the person you just matched with actually share.

You both get told

When it's mutual, both of you find out at the same time. No wondering whether a like landed, and nothing hidden behind an upgrade.

One neighbourhood at a time

An area opens only once there are enough people in it. Your first session is of a populated app, not an empty one you never come back to.

Your face, your rules

Privacy that is on by choice, not by settings menu

Blur your photos

Strangers see a blur, not your face. Reveal it when you match — or when someone asks and you decide they can.

Private photos, by request

Keep certain photos locked by default. Someone has to ask, and you decide who gets to see them — one person at a time, and never the algorithm.

Verified with a live gesture

The badge means someone actually checked. You're asked to hold a pose we pick on the spot, so a saved photo can't pass — and the selfie is deleted either way.

Screenshots blocked

Across every screen in the app, not just the ones with photos on them. What you show one person stays with that person.

Coarse location only

We never ask for your precise location, and what we do store is snapped to a grid before it is written down. Distance stays useful; your address stays yours.

Block, report, and leave

Blocking unmatches and hides in both directions, immediately. And if you want out, you can delete your account for real or export everything we hold on you.

More than dating

The same app, asking different questions

Circle

Who to go climbing with, not who to date. Its own deck, its own colour, and kept completely separate — nobody crosses between the two by accident.

Events

Real things happening near you, hosted by people on the app. Often the easier first step than a message — you turn up and the conversation is already happening.

Duos

Pair up with a friend and meet another pair. Lower stakes than a first date on your own, and nobody is ever added to one without agreeing to it first.

Passport

Moving city, or just visiting? Look ahead at where you're going and start talking before you land, without pretending you already live there.

Voice prompts

A few seconds of someone's voice tells you things a photo never will. Optional, on your profile, and nobody has to record one.

Chat that carries more

Photos, voice notes and a location you meant to send — not a wall of text. Everything inside the conversation, nothing leaking out of it.

Why "Wobblenest"?

A nest doesn't get built in one swipe. It takes a little wobble, some trial and error, and two people actually choosing to build something together. That's the kind of dating app we set out to make — one that's honest about the process, not just the outcome.
