1. Who we are

Wobblenest is operated by Venros Tech. In this policy "we" and "us" mean Venros Tech, and "you" means the person using the app. We are the party that decides what data the app collects and what happens to it.

For anything to do with your data — a question, a correction, a copy, a deletion, or a complaint — write to helpdesk@wobblenest.com.

3. What we do not do

These are not aspirations. They are statements about the software as it is today.

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • There is no analytics or tracking software in the app. No Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Sentry, no advertising SDK, no attribution or crash-reporting service. There is nothing in the app reporting your behaviour to a third party.
  • We do not currently serve advertising. If that changes we will update this policy and say so in the app before any ad code ships.
  • We do not read your conversations for advertising or profiling. We can access messages only where we have to — acting on a report, or complying with a legal order.
  • We never ask for your precise location and the app does not request that permission.
  • We do not store your verification selfie.

4. Who can see what

Other people using the app

Your name, age, photos that you have made public, city, approximate distance, and anything you have written on your profile are visible to people the app shows you to. Your exact date of birth, your phone number, and your location coordinates are never shown to another user.

Photos you mark private are not visible to anyone until you grant that person access, one person at a time, and you can revoke it. If you have blurring switched on, people who have not matched with you see a blurred version and nothing else until you decide otherwise.

Us

Access is limited to what is needed to run the service and to act on reports. Reviewing a verification request, or a report someone has filed, are the two routine reasons a person at Venros Tech looks at your content.

Third parties

Only the infrastructure providers in section 7, who process data on our instructions, and anyone we are legally required to disclose to under a valid order.

5. Location, specifically

The app asks only for approximate location, never precise. Android offers both; we request the coarse permission and block the fine one.

Beyond that, we deliberately destroy precision before writing anything down. Your position is snapped to a grid of roughly 550 metres at the moment it is stored, so the coordinates in our database are not your location — they are the corner of a large square that contains it. This is enforced in the database itself, on every path that can write a location.

We do this because rounding the answer is not enough: a distance shown to several people can be combined to work backwards to a point. Destroying the precision at the input means there is nothing accurate to work back to. Distances are also shown rounded, and never below one kilometre.

6. Photos and verification

Photos are held in object storage and served through links that expire. A photo you have not made public is not reachable by guessing a URL.

Verification works by asking you to take a selfie holding a gesture that we choose at that moment, so a saved photo cannot be used. That selfie is compared against your cover photo and then deleted as soon as the decision is made — whether you were verified or not. We keep the outcome, never the image. Changing your cover photo clears the badge, because the thing that was checked has changed.

Screenshots are blocked throughout the app. That is a real protection but not a perfect one — nothing can stop someone photographing a screen with another device. Please share accordingly.

7. Where your data is held

We use a small number of infrastructure providers, who process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase — the database, your account, and sign-in. This includes sending your one-time sign-in code, which is passed to an SMS provider for delivery.
  • Cloudflare R2 — photo and audio storage.
  • GitHub Pages — this website only. It holds no app data.

These providers operate infrastructure in several countries, so your information may be processed outside India. We choose providers that contractually commit to protecting it.

8. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open: for as long as you keep it.
  • When you delete your account: you are removed from the app immediately — every match ends, and you stop appearing to anyone. Your data is then permanently erased after 14 days. That gap exists so that a deletion made in anger, or by accident, can be undone. Within those 14 days you can cancel it; after them it cannot be recovered by us or by you.
  • Verification selfies: destroyed at the moment the check is decided.
  • Blocks and reports: kept after deletion where we need them to keep a blocked person from returning, or to act on a safety matter.

9. Your choices

All of these are in the app, under Settings, and none require writing to us:

  • Take a copy. Export everything we hold on you — your profile, photos, prompts, matches, messages, blocks and reports — as a single file.
  • Correct it. Every profile field can be edited or cleared.
  • Delete it. Delete your account, as described in section 8.
  • Hide your face. Blur your photos to anyone you have not matched with, and reveal them per person.
  • Block and report. Blocking is immediate and works in both directions.
  • Withdraw permissions. Location, camera and microphone can be revoked in your phone's settings; the features that need them stop, and the rest of the app keeps working.

If you would rather we did any of this for you, or you want to know something this page does not answer, write to helpdesk@wobblenest.com.

10. Age

Wobblenest is for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account, and the app is not directed at children. If we find an account belongs to someone under 18 we remove it. If you believe a minor is using the app, tell us at helpdesk@wobblenest.com and we will act.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is enforced in the database itself rather than only in the app, so a rule about who may read what cannot be bypassed by a modified client. Private photos and blurred photos are filtered on the server: the app is never sent an image the viewer is not entitled to see and told to hide it.

No service can promise it will never be breached. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as required by law.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. For a change that materially affects you — introducing advertising, for instance — we will tell you in the app before it takes effect, not afterwards.

13. Contact and complaints

Write to helpdesk@wobblenest.com. We aim to answer within a few days, and to resolve data requests within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a complaint, you may raise it with the data protection authority where you live.
